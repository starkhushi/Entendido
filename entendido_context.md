# Entendido - who we are (for the prospecting agents)

Entendido (Dehradun, India; about 10 people; entendido.org) designs behavioural learning
experiences for schools and organisations. The Spanish word means "intentional understanding".

## What we sell to organisations
- A diagnostic of how a leadership group fits together, built on Extended DISC
  (natural vs adapted style, pair assessment, team dynamics). We describe what the
  diagnostic shows; we do not lead with the tool name.
- Compass: individual and team version of the role-fitment / team-dynamics work.
- Short modules: Active Listening, Empathetic Accountability, Learning Agility, Drive,
  Self-Leadership, Navigating Workplace Conflict, Managing Cultural Transformation,
  Building Effective Communication, Resolving Conflict and Team Dysfunctions,
  Competency-based Candidate-Role Fitment.
- Senior Leadership Team (SLT) workshops, and the multi-year school programme "Thrive".

## Which offering fits which development (match the specific need, not the headline)
Do not default to "leadership integration". Read what the development changes for
people, then pick the offering that matches that change. Two different developments
should usually lead to two different needs.

| Development | What it usually changes for people | Offerings that fit |
|---|---|---|
| Merger, acquisition, integration | Two leadership groups must become one; unclear decision rights | Leadership-group diagnostic, SLT workshop, Managing Cultural Transformation |
| New CEO or unit head | A new leader and an inherited team must learn each other | Compass (leader-team pair fit), SLT workshop on working agreements |
| Franchise, partner or channel model | Managers must lead people they do not employ; influence without authority | Building Effective Communication, Empathetic Accountability, Self-Leadership for area or partner managers (we have not run partner programmes; flag as a stretch) |
| Rapid scaling, new sites, hiring surge | Many first-time managers; hiring for fit | Self-Leadership, Active Listening, Empathetic Accountability, Competency-based Candidate-Role Fitment |
| Restructuring, layoffs, attrition after a deal | Trust and conflict inside remaining teams | Navigating Workplace Conflict, Resolving Conflict and Team Dysfunctions |
| Pivot, new business line, digital or product shift | People must unlearn and relearn fast | Learning Agility, Drive, Managing Cultural Transformation |
| IPO or new investors | Leadership team under scrutiny; governance and pace | Leadership-group diagnostic, SLT workshop |

## Fit score framework (weights add to 100)
- Evidence of need (30): concrete, cited signs that a people or capability gap exists
  or is very likely. A big event alone is weak evidence.
- Capability fit (25): how well that specific need matches what we deliver. Lower it
  when strong internal L&D already covers the need, or when it is outside our
  experience (vendor, farmer or partner programmes).
- Decision-maker access (15): a named, reachable leader of a single unit. Lower it
  when only group HR or procurement is visible.
- Urgency (15): how recent and time-bound the trigger is. Last 6 months is high.
- Commercial feasibility (15): budget and size, procurement complexity, whether a
  10-person firm can win and deliver it.
Each is scored 0 to 5 with evidence. The weighted total is out of 5:
High 3.5 and above, Medium 2.5 to 3.4, Low below 2.5.

## The prospecting rule (from our own briefs)
We look for companies with a big job to do AND a leadership group that has recently
changed shape: M&A or integration, founder/CEO/CHRO exit, new CEO, IPO, market entry,
regulatory or trade change, rapid scaling, attrition after a deal.
The research does NOT establish that a problem exists. Facts are facts; interpretations
are guesses and must be labelled "derived".

## Fit rules
- Sell to a single leadership group (a subsidiary, a unit, a new team), not to group HR.
- Large groups have HR bureaucracies and empanelled vendors that swallow us. Avoid
  pitches that go to procurement as a "training purchase".
- Watch for "we already do this" (internal development centres). Our counter: those
  assess individuals against a standard; we map how the group works together.
- We have not worked with farmer or vendor programmes; team-level pieces suit us best.
- Legal matters, lawsuits, allegations: know, never mention unless they raise it.
