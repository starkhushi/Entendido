# Entendido - who we are (for the prospecting agents)

Entendido (Dehradun, India; about 10 people; entendido.org) designs behavioural learning
experiences for schools and organisations. The Spanish word means "intentional understanding".

## What we sell to organisations
- A diagnostic of how a leadership group fits together, built on Extended DISC
  (natural vs adapted style, pair assessment, team dynamics). We describe what the
  diagnostic shows; we do not lead with the tool name.
- Compass: individual and team version of the role-fitment / team-dynamics work.
- Short modules: Active Listening, Empathetic Accountability, Learning Agility, Drive,
  Self-Leadership, Navigating Workplace Conflict, Managing Cultural Transformation,
  Building Effective Communication, Resolving Conflict and Team Dysfunctions,
  Competency-based Candidate-Role Fitment.
- Senior Leadership Team (SLT) workshops, and the multi-year school programme "Thrive".

## The prospecting rule (from our own briefs)
We look for companies with a big job to do AND a leadership group that has recently
changed shape: M&A or integration, founder/CEO/CHRO exit, new CEO, IPO, market entry,
regulatory or trade change, rapid scaling, attrition after a deal.
The research does NOT establish that a problem exists. Facts are facts; interpretations
are guesses and must be labelled "derived".

## Fit rules
- Sell to a single leadership group (a subsidiary, a unit, a new team), not to group HR.
- Large groups have HR bureaucracies and empanelled vendors that swallow us. Avoid
  pitches that go to procurement as a "training purchase".
- Watch for "we already do this" (internal development centres). Our counter: those
  assess individuals against a standard; we map how the group works together.
- We have not worked with farmer or vendor programmes; team-level pieces suit us best.
- Legal matters, lawsuits, allegations: know, never mention unless they raise it.
